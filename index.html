Donate · Donaciones

This year, we're funding paraeducators.

Paraeducators are everywhere your kid is — in classrooms, on buses, at recess, at lunch. More para support is the number one thing our community asked for: 69% of our teachers and 91% of our parents. Our goal is $120,000, enough to secure two full-time paraeducator positions for the 2026–27 school year.

$124,368 came in from families and our community last cycle — and it stretches further than it looks. Every Uni Hill student gets free breakfast and lunch, and this year the PTA covers the glue, paper and pencils other schools ask parents to buy, bulk-ordered under our tax-exempt status.

Uni Hill PTA is a registered non-profit (Tax ID 32-0067584), so your donation is tax-deductible. Check whether your employer matches donations — it can double your gift.
